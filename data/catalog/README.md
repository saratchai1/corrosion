# catalogs
Scene catalogs are populated by live yearly paginated STAC searches. They retain selected candidates, AOI quality fields, provenance and local-download metadata when a sample is acquired.

`project_samut_songkhram_sentinel2_scenes.csv` records the 12 January–April acquisitions used for the nine-plot 2024 planting assessment. Their AOI-only COGs are stored locally, not in Git.
